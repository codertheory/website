## What I collect

As little as possible. This site uses no analytics, no third-party trackers, and no cookies beyond what's strictly required to remember your theme preference. The contact form sends your message to my inbox — that's it.

## About my apps

Each app I publish has its own data story, and I try to keep them all simple: data lives on your device or in your iCloud account. Nothing flows back to me unless you opt in to crash reports or you reach out to me directly.

## Kids

None of my apps are designed for children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through one of my apps, contact me and I'll remove it.

## Your rights

You can ask me what I have on you (almost certainly nothing), ask me to delete it, or ask any other question about how your data is handled. I will answer.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated date at the top will move. Material changes will also be mentioned in a blog post.
